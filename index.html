Research question: how can we distinguish high-volume scoring from efficient scoring? This reproducible demonstration uses fictional player profiles, not official NBA statistics.

HYPOTHESIS

MORE POINTS ≠ MORE EFFICIENCY

High scoring volume may coexist with lower shooting efficiency. Comparing PPG alongside true shooting percentage helps avoid rewarding volume alone.

TS% = points / [2 × (field goal attempts + 0.44 × free throw attempts)].

METHOD & FINDING

COMPARE THE RIGHT DENOMINATORS

The sample profiles deliberately separate volume from efficiency. In the demo, Orion Vale scores the most, while Jules Mercer has the highest TS%.

This is a methodological demonstration, not an empirical conclusion about NBA players.
