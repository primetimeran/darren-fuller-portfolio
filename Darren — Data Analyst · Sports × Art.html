Analyst Principles

01

Context beats stats

A number without a story is just a stat line. Always ask "compared to what?"

02

Make it felt

If a chart doesn't make you lean in, redesign it. Clarity can still have drama.

03

Respect the data's history

Know where it came from, how it was collected, and what it can't tell you.

04

Insight must move

Analysis that doesn't change a decision is just decoration.
